# Working notes for this repo

Project-specific conventions. See also README.md (what the site is), coverage.md
(source status), roadmap.md (plan) and outreach.md (the griffie/vendor mails).

## Drafting e-mails (outreach.md and any mail proposed in chat)

These are real mails sent by the maintainer to Statengriffies and vendors, so they
are written to his preferences, not mine:

- **No em dashes (—) in mail text. Use a comma instead.** This applies to the Dutch
  mail bodies, including drafts proposed in chat before they are logged here. The
  editorial notes *around* a draft may still use them.
  En dashes (–) in bullet lists, like `– Website: ...`, are fine and stay.
- **Never offer or ask for a phone call, and do not include a phone number.** The
  maintainer does not want to call or be called. Offer to answer questions by mail
  instead. If a correspondent gives a phone number, do not reuse or repeat it.
- Keep correspondents' **direct** contact details (personal e-mail, mobile) out of
  this repo. It is public. Role and name are fine; put direct details in the private
  memory notes instead.
- House style that has worked (three vendors/griffies fixed a block within a day):
  name the exact URL, say what still works, offer two concrete options rather than
  describing a problem, quantify the load, and link the live site plus the source.
  Write for the supplier as the real audience, because the griffie forwards it.

## Access and blocks

Never try to defeat an anti-bot measure (Anubis, a Cloudflare challenge, a geo or
datacenter block). Ask instead; that is what has actually worked here. See
coverage.md "Status van de bronnen" for the current blocks.
